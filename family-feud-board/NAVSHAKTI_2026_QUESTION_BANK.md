### Scoring & Stage Mechanics:
1. **Survey Points**: Each question features 5 distinct surveyed answer themes totaling **100 points**.
2. **Active Turn**: Whichever team wins the face-off controls the board.
3. **Strikes & Steals**:
   - 3 incorrect guesses = **3 STRIKES**.
   - Upon 3 strikes, the host triggers a **STEAL OPPORTUNITY** for the opposing team.
   - The opposing team has **ONE combined guess** to name any remaining unrevealed answer on the board.
   - If successful, the stealing team steals the entire accumulated Round Bank!
4. **Data Disclosure**: All questions in this bank use provisional demo point values until final pre-event campus surveys are collected from SOC students and faculty.

## 💡 Quick Tips for the Event Emcee
1. **Pacing**: Give contestants 5 to 7 seconds to think. Don't rush reveals—let the competitive banter build tension!
2. **Steal Dramatic Pause**: When a team hits 3 strikes, shout: *"And that is THREE STRIKES! The board is wide open for a STEAL!"* before clicking the Steal button.
3. **Audience Clues**: Encourage the auditorium crowd to cheer or shout when the emcee calls: *"SURVEY SAYS...!"* before pressing the <kbd>Space</kbd> key.
